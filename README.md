## ✨ Features

- 📺 **Full Web Video Engine**: Watch YouTube, Twitch, Lo-Fi streams, and custom web video links directly on your CarPlay display.
- 📱 **Screen Mirroring & AirPlay**: AirPlay streaming tunnel to mirror your phone screen or local video files.
- 🎛️ **Native CarPlay Interface**: Built using native with steering wheel and media control integration.
- 🔓 **Free Apple ID Sideloading**: Pre-configured so the app icon displays directly on your CarPlay dashboard without requiring a $99/yr Apple Developer Account.

## 📲 How to Install on iPhone

1. Download **[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/)** (or AltStore )
2. Connect your iPhone to your PC via USB cable.
3. Drag and drop **`CarStream.ipa`** into Sideloadly.
4. Enter your **Free Apple ID** email address and click **Start**.

### Mandatory iOS Post-Installation Setup:
> [!IMPORTANT]
> 1. **Trust Certificate**: Go to `Settings` > `General` > `VPN & Device Management` > Tap your Apple ID > Tap **Trust**.
> 2. **Developer Mode (iOS 16+)**: Go to `Settings` > `Privacy & Security` > Scroll down to **Developer Mode** > Turn **ON** and restart iPhone.
